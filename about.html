Who We AreOverview

Pune's Industrial Maintenance Partner

Omshree Industries Service is a Pune-based industrial solutions company dedicated to one purpose: keeping manufacturing operations running smoothly. We provide mechanical and electrical maintenance, breakdown recovery, installation support, and skilled industrial manpower to manufacturing plants and factories across Pune and Pimpri Chinchwad (PCMC).

Founded and led by Pratibha Pandhari Wadje, the company began with a focused mission — to offer the kind of reliable, technically skilled industrial support that smaller and mid-scale manufacturing units in Pune often struggle to find.

What sets us apart is not just technical capability, but our commitment to showing up, following through, and building genuine long-term working relationships with every client we serve.

Our JourneyCompany Story

How We Got Here

From a focused service team to a trusted industrial partner — our journey has been built one satisfied client at a time.

THE BEGINNING

Founded in Pune

Omshree Industries Service was founded with a clear vision: to provide technically skilled, dependable maintenance support to the manufacturing sector in Pune and PCMC — a region with one of India's highest concentrations of engineering and auto component manufacturers.

EARLY GROWTH

Expanding Service Capability

Starting with mechanical breakdown support, the company quickly expanded into electrical maintenance and machine installation services. The team grew alongside client demand, adding qualified technicians for CNC, VMC, HMC, and lathe machine servicing.

MANPOWER DIVISION

Launching Industrial Workforce Solutions

Recognising a clear need among manufacturing clients for reliable skilled and semi-skilled workers, the company launched its dedicated manpower solutions division — supplying operators, fitters, welders, electricians, and production staff on contract basis.

TODAY

Your Full-Service Industrial Partner

Today, Omshree Industries Service offers comprehensive mechanical maintenance, electrical maintenance, and manpower solutions to manufacturing companies across multiple industries — all backed by the same commitment to quality and reliability that has defined us since day one.

LeadershipOur Proprietor

Pratibha Pandhari Wadje

Proprietor & Director, Omshree Industries Service

Pratibha Pandhari Wadje leads Omshree Industries Service with a hands-on approach, strong technical understanding, and an unwavering focus on client satisfaction. His leadership has been central to building a company that manufacturing units across Pune can genuinely rely on — not just for one-off repairs, but as a long-term operational partner.

He built the company around three principles that continue to guide every engagement: technical honesty, professional execution, and reliable follow-through. Under his direction, the company has grown from offering targeted breakdown maintenance into a full-spectrum industrial services provider covering maintenance, installation, and manpower.

Pratibha is deeply committed to the belief that manufacturing businesses — especially small and mid-scale units — deserve the same quality of industrial support as large enterprises, without the overhead of retaining full-time technical staff for every function.

What Drives Us

ValuesWhat We Stand For

Our Core Values

Every interaction, every job, every technician we deploy is guided by these four principles.

01

Reliability

We show up when we say we will, complete the work to the agreed standard, and follow up to ensure everything is functioning correctly. Reliability is not an aspiration for us — it is our baseline.

02

Technical Excellence

We believe every machine deserves the right diagnosis, the right repair, and the right technician. We invest in skilled people and use the correct methods — not shortcuts — to get the job done properly.

03

Transparency

Honest assessment, clear scope of work, and no hidden costs. We tell clients exactly what needs to be done and why — then we do exactly that. Trust is built one honest interaction at a time.

04

Safety First

All our on-site work is carried out following safe working practices. We take plant safety seriously — not just because it is required, but because it reflects the professionalism we bring to every engagement.
