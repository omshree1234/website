FAQCommon Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we receive most often from manufacturing companies before engaging with us.

What areas do you cover for machine maintenance?

▾

We primarily cover Pune and Pimpri Chinchwad (PCMC) — including all major MIDC zones such as Bhosari, Chinchwad, Chakan, Ranjangaon, Hadapsar, Talegaon, and surrounding industrial areas. For locations outside this primary area, contact us to confirm availability.

How quickly can you respond to a machine breakdown?

▾

Response time depends on the nature of the fault and your location, but we treat breakdown calls as high priority and aim to have a technician on-site as quickly as possible. For urgent breakdowns, calling or WhatsApping us directly gives the fastest response — please do not rely only on the online form for urgent situations.

What types of machines do you service?

▾

We cover a wide range of industrial machinery including CNC, VMC, HMC, lathe, SPM, milling, industrial washing machines, furnaces, EOT cranes, hydraulic and pneumatic systems, drill machines, shot blasting equipment, paint shop machinery, cooling water systems, and blowers and motors. If your machine type is not listed, contact us — we can confirm whether our team has the required experience.

Can you supply manpower for both short-term and long-term requirements?

▾

Yes. We supply industrial manpower on daily, weekly, monthly, and rolling long-term contract terms. Whether you need two operators for a two-week production peak or a team of ten workers on an ongoing six-month contract, we can structure our engagement accordingly.

What is the process for requesting a preventive maintenance program?

▾

Contact us with details of your facility, the machines you want included in a preventive programme, and your preferred service frequency. We will assess your requirements and propose a maintenance schedule that fits around your production calendar. You can start with a pilot on one or two machines and expand from there.

Do you provide both mechanical and electrical maintenance?

▾

Yes. Omshree Industries Service covers both mechanical and electrical maintenance — including motor servicing, control panel inspection, sensor and limit switch work, wiring, and electrical breakdown response, in addition to all mechanical maintenance services. This dual capability means you have one partner for both domains.

How do I get a quote for maintenance or manpower services?

▾

You can request a quote by filling the enquiry form on this page, calling us on +91 9545318160, or sending a WhatsApp message with your requirement. For maintenance, please describe the machine type, fault or service needed, and your location. For manpower, please specify the roles, skill level, number of workers, and duration required.

Can I trust the workers you supply in my facility?

▾

We supply workers who have been assessed for the skills and experience relevant to the role. All personnel deployed through us are aware of basic industrial safety norms. We remain in contact throughout any deployment and handle replacement promptly if a worker is unsuitable or unavailable. Your plant's operational integrity is our responsibility.
