Supporting Pune's Manufacturing Sector

The Pune and Pimpri Chinchwad industrial belt is one of India's most significant manufacturing hubs — home to thousands of engineering, automotive, pharmaceutical, and general manufacturing companies. Omshree Industries Service was built to serve this ecosystem, providing the maintenance expertise and industrial manpower that keeps these factories productive and operational.
